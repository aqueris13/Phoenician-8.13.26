Scrub Bot Express

Two sites, Phoenix metro area · Acquired January 2022

Cobblestone acquired the Scrub Bot Express portfolio, two sites in the Phoenix metro (5324 N 16th St, Phoenix; 3965 W Ray Rd, Chandler), in January 2022. Based on public, mobile-location visit data (Advan), estimated monthly volume across the group increased 92% year-over-year on average, comparing the 12 months before acquisition to the first 12 months under Cobblestone ownership.
