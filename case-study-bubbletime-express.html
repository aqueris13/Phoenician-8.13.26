Bubble Time Express

Two sites, Thornton, CO · Acquired Apr to May 2022

Cobblestone acquired the Bubble Time Express portfolio, two sites in Thornton, CO (1600 E 104th Ave and 12105 Grant St), in April and May 2022. Based on public, mobile-location visit data, estimated monthly volume across the group increased 26% year-over-year on average, comparing the 12 months before acquisition to the first 12 months under Cobblestone ownership.
