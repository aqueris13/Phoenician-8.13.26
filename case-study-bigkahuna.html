Big Kahuna

2380 S Chambers Rd, Aurora, CO 80014 · Acquired December 2020

Cobblestone acquired Big Kahuna (2380 S Chambers Rd, Aurora, CO 80014) in December 2020. The site was closed from January 2021 through August 2023. Based on public, mobile-location visit data (AlphaMap), estimated monthly volume since reopening has held in the 180-190% range above the 2020 pre-acquisition baseline.
